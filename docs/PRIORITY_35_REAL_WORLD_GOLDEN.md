# Priority 35 — Real-World Golden Scenarios

Priority 35 adds deterministic scenario coverage for a realistic concrete-building workflow.

Scenarios:
- residential concrete quantity flow across columns, beams and slab
- estimate recalculation plus project backup/recovery
- multi-surface golden acceptance (takeoff, estimate, performance)
- 120-row pagination / larger-project behavior

These are offline deterministic acceptance scenarios. They do not claim coverage of every UI path, external file, or real customer dataset.
