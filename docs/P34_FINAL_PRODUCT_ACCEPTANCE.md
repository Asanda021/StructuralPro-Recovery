# P34 — Final Product Acceptance

## Purpose
P34 is the final product-acceptance gate before P35 FINAL SIGN-OFF.

It verifies that the repository has a complete, deterministic evidence chain for
the master roadmap and that all final acceptance surfaces are explicitly covered.

## Acceptance surfaces
1. Functional
2. UI/UX
3. Persian / RTL
4. Takeoff
5. CAD
6. BIM
7. AI
8. Estimate
9. Commercial
10. Reports
11. Performance
12. Security
13. Regression
14. Competitive parity

## Evidence rule
P15 through P33 artifacts are required. Missing evidence fails the gate.
The gate is fail-closed and deterministic.

## Important boundary
A green P34 result means the defined StructuralPro acceptance contract is
complete and internally evidenced. It does **not** fabricate customer,
third-party, or external competitor test evidence.

P35 remains separate and is intentionally not claimed by P34.
